Frequently Asked Questions

Can I put personal items in my car?

Yes, you may place up to 100 lbs of personal items in the trunk or cargo area of the vehicle. Items must be secured in a box or suitcase. Note that the carrier's insurance does not cover personal items, so avoid packing valuables, electronics, or hazardous materials.

What is the difference between open and enclosed transport?

Open transport is the industry standard—it is safe, cost-effective, and exposes your car to the same elements it would face driving on the highway. Enclosed transport utilizes a fully covered trailer to protect high-value, exotic, or classic vehicles from all weather, dust, and road debris.

How long does auto transport take?

Transit times depend heavily on the total distance. Regional routes (up to 500 miles) typically take 1-2 days. Mid-range routes (500-1500 miles) take 3-5 days. Cross-country routes (2,000+ miles) take 5-7 days. We provide guaranteed pickup windows and real-time GPS tracking for peace of mind.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes. Every carrier in our network is required to carry comprehensive cargo insurance. In the rare event of damage, your vehicle is fully covered with zero deductible to you from pickup to delivery.
