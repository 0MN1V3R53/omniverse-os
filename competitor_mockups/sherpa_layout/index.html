Common Questions

How does the Price Lock Promise work?

When you book with Sky Auto Services, we utilize market data to guarantee the transport rate. If the market shifts and a carrier demands more money to move the vehicle, we cover up to $300 of the difference out of our own revenue to ensure your car moves without you paying a penny more than quoted.

What happens if my vehicle is damaged?

Damage is exceedingly rare because of our stringent carrier vetting. However, if damage does occur, your vehicle is covered under the carrier's cargo insurance policy from bumper to bumper with zero deductible to you.

Can I pack items in my trunk?

Yes, you may load up to 100 lbs of personal items in the trunk or cargo area. Items must be secured. Note that carrier insurance does not cover personal belongings.

When do I pay?

Unlike other brokers, we charge absolutely $0 upfront. A small deposit is charged to your card only once a carrier is secured and dispatched. The remaining balance is paid directly to the driver at delivery via cash or certified funds.
