1\. The Rosettes Fold-Out: Topography & Sanctuary
-------------------------------------------------

For centuries, Folio 86v—a massive 6-panel fold-out featuring nine interconnected circular nodes—was believed to be a map of the cosmos, the Milky Way, or a magical realm. Leviathan's translation of the micro-text along the connecting pathways reveals it is a physical, terrestrial map.

### Folio 86v (f86v) - The Sanctuary Network Map

ORIGINAL VISUAL: Nine massive circular "rosettes" connected by causeways, featuring castles, stars, and flowing water textures.  
RECOVERED SHORTHAND PHONETICS: \[Hot-Spring\] \[Mountain-Pass\] \[Guild-Safehouse\] \[Guard-Avoid\]  
  
TRANSLATION OF THE CENTRAL NODE (The Great Spring):  
"The Mother Basin. Here the waters boil from the deep earth, smelling of sulfur and salt. It is the primary sanctuary for the winter months. The causeway to the North \[Upper Left Rosette\] leads through the high alpine pass—impassable from the month of the Wolf until the Thaw. The castles depicted on the Eastern path \[Right Rosettes\] mark the toll-gates of the Duke's men. Do not carry the raw Belladonna through these gates; they will burn you as a poisoner. Travel the subterranean cave route \[Lower connecting tubes\] to reach the Southern apothecary unharmed."  
  
LEVIATHAN CONTEXTUAL ANALYSIS:  
The Rosettes are a covert topological map of nine thermal hot springs in the Italian Dolomites and Swiss Alps. It served as a safe-route guide for the guild of healers to transport medicinal compounds while avoiding Inquisition patrols and regional tax collectors.  

2\. Advanced Pharmacopoeia: Anesthesia & Toxins
-----------------------------------------------

The guild did not just treat fevers; they performed minor surgeries and managed extreme pain during catastrophic childbirths. This required highly dangerous compounds that walked the line between medicine and lethal poison.

### Folio 33v (f33v) - The Muscle Spasm (Nightshade)

ORIGINAL VISUAL: A dark-leafed plant with dark, berry-like clusters and an aggressive, twisting root.  
RECOVERED SHORTHAND PHONETICS: \[Berry-Black\] \[Crush-Dilute\] \[Muscle-Release\] \[Eye-Widen\]  
  
TRANSLATION:  
"The Devil's Cherry \[Belladonna\]. When the body locks itself in violent spasms, or the jaw clamps shut from the rust-sickness \[Tetanus\], this must be used with the utmost terror. Crush three black berries—no more—and dilute into a full skin of wine. It forces the muscles to surrender and widens the dark of the eyes. If the patient swallows the un-diluted juice, their skin will burn dry, their mind will fill with waking nightmares, and their heart will stop before sunset. Keep far from the reach of the unlearned."  

### Folio 89r (f89r) - The Deep Sleep Sponge (Surgical Anesthesia)

ORIGINAL VISUAL: Several decorated cylindrical apothecary jars arranged near small, fibrous clumps.  
RECOVERED SHORTHAND PHONETICS: \[Sponge-Sea\] \[Poppy-Sap\] \[Henbane-Boil\] \[Breathe-Deep\]  
  
TRANSLATION:  
"The Sponge of Lethe. For when the flesh must be cut or the shattered bone must be reset. Boil the sap of the Opium Poppy, the root of the Mandrake, and the seeds of Henbane in strong vinegar. Submerge a clean sea-sponge into the boiling mixture until it drinks the liquid entirely. Dry the sponge in the sun. When the knife is required, soak the sponge in hot water and hold it firmly over the patient's nose and mouth. They will fall into a sleep as deep as death and feel no iron. To wake them, you must drag a sponge soaked in pure, sharp vinegar across their lips and nose."
