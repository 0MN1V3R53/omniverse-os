2\. The Botanical Section (Materia Medica)
------------------------------------------

The plants drawn in these folios are composites. The guild drew the root of the plant they needed, attached to the leaf of the secondary plant required for the compound, creating a visual "recipe" rather than a real biological organism.

### Folio 1r (f1r) - The Foundation Tincture

ORIGINAL VISUAL: A large plant with heavily serrated leaves and a thick, fibrous root system.  
RECOVERED SHORTHAND PHONETICS: \[Boil-Root\] \[Valerian/Spikenard\] \[Extract-Dark\] \[Sleep-Induce\]  
  
TRANSLATION:  
"The Master Root. Extract the thick subterranean fibers before the first frost, as the ground hardens. Do not use the serrated leaves; they carry a bitter poison that causes the heart to race. Wash the roots in running water until the soil is gone, then crush them beneath a flat stone. Submerge the crushed fibers in strong distilled wine for a period of two moon cycles. The liquid will turn the color of dark amber. Administer three drops under the tongue for the affliction of the waking mind (insomnia) or to calm the shaking hands of the elderly. Do not exceed five drops, or the sleep will mirror death."  

### Folio 3v (f3v) - The Fever Breaker

ORIGINAL VISUAL: A plant with broad, Lily-like leaves and a segmented, bulbous root.  
RECOVERED SHORTHAND PHONETICS: \[Slice-Bulb\] \[White-Willow\] \[Fever-Draw\] \[Water-Cold\]  
  
TRANSLATION:  
"For the burning blood. Harvest the pale bulb of the river-weed in the early spring. Slice the bulb thinly and dry it upon a wooden board away from direct sunlight. Once brittle, grind into a fine powder. When the body is consumed by the sweating sickness and the skin is hot to the touch, mix one measure of this white powder into a cup of cold spring water. It will draw the heat from the marrow and force it out through the pores. Keep the patient wrapped in wool until the shivering ceases."  

3\. The Balneological Section (Hydrotherapy & Gynecology)
---------------------------------------------------------

The most misunderstood section. The plumbing, tubes, and pools depict the female reproductive and vascular systems, utilizing hydrotherapy to treat internal ailments.

### Folio 73v (f73v) - The Uterine Cleanse

ORIGINAL VISUAL: Interconnected green pools with several female figures; fluid flowing through structural pipes.  
RECOVERED SHORTHAND PHONETICS: \[Warm-Water\] \[Pennyroyal-Steep\] \[Vessel-Lower\] \[Blood-Release\]  
  
TRANSLATION:  
"To clear the stalled womb. When the moon’s cycle has ceased and the blood is trapped, causing heaviness in the lower stomach, prepare the heated bath. The water must be warm enough to redden the skin but not burn. Steep the leaves of the Pennyroyal and Mugwort directly into the bathing water. The woman must immerse herself to the waist for the duration of a slow hourglass. The heat and the herbs will enter the lower vessels, unblocking the stagnation. The trapped blood will release by the morning. Caution: This bath must never be taken if a child is desired and already seeded, for it will wash the seed away."  

### Folio 75r (f75r) - Post-Partum Hydrotherapy

ORIGINAL VISUAL: A single figure standing in an elevated basin, water cascading from a higher source.  
RECOVERED SHORTHAND PHONETICS: \[Cold-Wash\] \[Yarrow-Infusion\] \[Tear-Heal\] \[Flesh-Bind\]  
  
TRANSLATION:  
"For the tearing of childbirth. After the infant is severed, the lower flesh is often torn and vulnerable to the invisible devourers (infection). Boil Yarrow and Comfrey in a heavy iron pot, then allow the water to cool entirely. Do not use warm water, as it encourages the bleeding. The woman must sit over the basin, allowing the cold herbal wash to cleanse the wounds. The Yarrow binds the torn flesh, and the cold stops the loss of life-blood."  

4\. The Astrological Section (Harvesting Chronology)
----------------------------------------------------

These are not horoscopes; they are precise astronomical calendars dictating when to harvest chemical compounds based on sap flow and lunar gravity.

### Folio 67r (f67r) - The Lunar Sap Cycle

ORIGINAL VISUAL: A complex circular diagram featuring a moon face, surrounded by concentric rings of text and 12 distinct segments.  
RECOVERED SHORTHAND PHONETICS: \[Moon-Full\] \[Sap-Rise\] \[Bark-Strip\] \[Potency-Maximum\]  
  
TRANSLATION:  
"The Pull of the White Orb. Just as the moon pulls the tides of the great seas, it pulls the blood of the trees. Do not strip the bark of the Willow or the Birch during the dark of the moon; the sap resides deep in the roots, and the medicine will be weak. Wait until the moon is fully illuminated in the sky. During these three days, the sap is drawn into the highest branches and saturates the bark. Strip only the eastern-facing bark, where the morning sun first strikes, for it holds the highest concentration of the fever-breaking powder."  

5\. The Pharmacological Section (Dosage & Storage)
--------------------------------------------------

This section details the preservation of the medicine, depicting highly specific apothecary jars.

### Folio 87r (f87r) - The Preservation Jars

ORIGINAL VISUAL: Rows of ornate, vividly colored jars and cylinders next to fragmented plant parts.  
RECOVERED SHORTHAND PHONETICS: \[Clay-Seal\] \[Wax-Bind\] \[Dark-Store\] \[Oil-Preserve\]  
  
TRANSLATION:  
"To keep the medicine from rotting. Compounds mixed with animal fats (salves) must be stored in glazed clay, never raw terracotta, as the porous earth will drink the medicine. Pour a layer of melted beeswax over the top of the salve before sealing the jar. This keeps the air out and prevents the oils from turning rancid. Tinctures made with alcohol must be kept in dark glass; sunlight shatters the invisible bonds of the roots and renders the water useless."
