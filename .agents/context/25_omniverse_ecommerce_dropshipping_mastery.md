## 3. Automated 48h–72h Product Pruning Protocol

  - Profit per Visitor (EPV): $\ge \$1.10$.
- **Action Matrix**:
  - *Breakout Winners ($\text{ROAS} \ge 3.0\text{x}$)*: Scale budget by $+30\%$ every 48 hours; create 3 new UGC hook variations.
  - *Average Performers ($2.0\text{x} \le \text{ROAS} < 3.0\text{x}$)*: Optimize product page copy and test bundle discounts.
  - *Underperformers ($\text{ROAS} < 2.0\text{x}$)*: Auto-prune SKU from hero placement within 48 hours; rotate in next researched winning product.
