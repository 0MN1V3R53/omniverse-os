## Understanding the Google Crawler

Now that the pages are built, we wait for the **Google Crawler** (also known as the Google Spider). 

<div class="box">
    <h3>What is the Crawler?</h3>
    <p>Google uses automated bots (spiders) to read and organize the entire internet. When we launch 3,148 new pages, we submit an "XML Sitemap" (a digital map) to Google, inviting their spiders to scan your site.</p>
</div>

### The Timeline
- **Discovery (Weeks 1-2):** The spider visits the site, finds the 3,148 pages, and adds them to Google's database.
- **Indexing (Weeks 2-6):** Google evaluates the speed, structure, and quality of the pages. Because your site uses Next.js and loads instantly, Google will rate it highly.
- **Ranking (Months 2-6):** Over time, as users click on your pages and stay to use the Quote Calculator, Google pushes your pages higher up the search results.

**SEO is a marathon, not a sprint.** The infrastructure is perfectly laid; the spiders are working.
