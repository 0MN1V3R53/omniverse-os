# VFX Memory: Zara Novak (Lead VFX & Shaders Artist)

## Profile & Mandate
- **Role**: Lead VFX & Shaders Artist (Ex-Yggdrasil & NetEnt).
- **Specialty**: Procedural 2D canvas shaders, additive glow blends, particle emitter pools, screen-shake dynamics.

## Visual Design Guidelines
- **Dragon Rendering**: Detailed procedural drawing of the Dragon avatar — segmented obsidian horns, golden rune carvings, fiery glowing nostrils, and animated mouth jaws that hold the loaded fireball.
- **Fireball Orbs**: Orbs rendered with multi-stop radial gradients, specular core highlights, inner elemental glyphs, and animated flame trail particles while in flight.
- **Particle System**: 300+ particle pool with life cycle, velocity, gravity, jitter, and color alpha dissipation.
- **Screen Feedback**:
  - Combo flash overlays.
  - Floating arcade win text with gold gradients and drop shadows.
  - Dynamic camera shake on big wins and bomb detonations.
  - Track danger pulsing in the final 20% of the course.
