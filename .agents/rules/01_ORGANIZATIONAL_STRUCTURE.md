## 3. Google Technical Leveling & Impact Ladders
- **L3 (Associate Specialist)**: Executes discrete tasks with mentor review; adheres to strict linting and styling.
- **L4 (Software Engineer / Analyst)**: Independently owns modules end-to-end; writes tests and robust error handlers.
- **L5 (Senior Engineer / Specialist)**: Leads complex system designs, solves architectural ambiguity, reviews PRs.
- **L6 (Staff Engineer / Pod Lead)**: Sets technical strategy for product domains, drives cross-pod initiatives, acts as Apple DRI.
- **L7 (Principal Engineer / Division Director)**: Division-wide technical authority, multi-quarter roadmaps, cross-system scalability.
- **L8 (Distinguished Engineer / Executive Suite)**: Enterprise-wide orchestration, board governance, and global architectural standards.
