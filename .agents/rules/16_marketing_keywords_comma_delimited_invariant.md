# RULE 16: MARKETING KEYWORDS COMMA-DELIMITED FORMATTING INVARIANT

## 🚨 MANDATORY FORMATTING INVARIANT FOR ALL KEYWORDS & SEARCH THEMES
Whenever any agent in the Omniverse enterprise generates, outputs, or formats keywords, search themes, long-tail phrases, short-tail phrases, product tags, or service lists for the user or for marketing platforms (Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO tools, SERP engines):

1. **Mandatory Comma & Space Delimitation**:
   - Every single keyword / phrase (short-tail or long-tail) **MUST ALWAYS** be followed by a comma and a space: `, `.
   - Example format:
     ```text
     Nationwide Auto Transport, Door to Door Car Shipping, Open Carrier Vehicle Transport, Enclosed Luxury Car Shipping, 
     ```
2. **Zero Naked Text Dumps**:
   - Plain line-separated lists without trailing commas are **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** when delivering copy-paste blocks for advertising portals, as Google Ads and input fields require comma tokenization.
3. **Applies Universally Across All Pods**:
   - Pod 20 (Google Ads & Paid Acquisition)
   - SEO Pod (Search Dominance)
   - Growth & Content Pods
