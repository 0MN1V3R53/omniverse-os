**Thread:** How much should I expect to pay for enclosed shipping on a 2024 Porsche 911 GT3 from NY to Miami?
**Intent:** High Buyer Intent | **Topic:** Luxury / Exotic Enclosed Auto Transport
**Author:** @SkyAuto_Logistics_Pat
**Response:**
> For a precision vehicle like a 911 GT3 on the NY -> South Florida snowbird corridor (approx. 1,280 road miles), standard open transport averages $950-$1,150, but for exotic low-clearance vehicles you definitely want soft-tie hydraulic liftgate enclosed transport. Enclosed rates typically run 1.4x to 1.6x open rates (~$1,550-$1,850 depending on season). Make sure your carrier is licensed with the FMCSA and provides a direct certificate of insurance (COI) with primary cargo coverage. ### Platform: TeslaMotorsClub (Model Y / Cybertruck Logistics)
**Thread:** Shipping an EV across the country - Any weight surcharges or battery state-of-charge rules?
**Intent:** Technical Logistics Query | **Topic:** EV Transport Regulations
**Author:** @EV_AutoLogistics_Pro
**Response:**
> Great question regarding EV shipping! Two major factors to keep in mind: 1) State of Charge: carriers typically require your battery to be between 25% and 50% SOC for safety during transit and to prevent parasitic drain. 2) Curb Weight: EVs are heavier due to battery packs (a dual-motor Model Y is ~4,400 lbs, Cybertruck ~6,600 lbs). Most transport companies apply a standard EV/Heavy surcharge (around +$350 for heavy EVs) to stay within federal gross vehicle weight (GVW) limits. Full guide on EV shipping requirements: https://skyautoservices.com/usa-auto-transport-news/door-to-door-auto-transport-cost-timeline-2026.
