## 3. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Reply Playbook

Every comment or forum reply generated by the team must follow the **4-Pillar Value-First Structure**:

### Pillar 1: Direct, Helpful Answer (Zero Fluff)
- Immediately address the user's specific query (e.g. exact pricing ballpark, expected transit days, carrier type recommendation).
- Acknowledge their specific origin, destination, and vehicle model without generic greetings.

### Pillar 2: Technical & Specialized Guidance
- Provide actionable logistics details tailored to their vehicle class:
  - **Exotics / Sports Cars:** Liftgate ramp angles, enclosed weather protection, over-the-tire soft-tie strapping.
  - **Electric Vehicles (EVs):** State-of-charge (SOC) 25%–50% safety rule, battery weight considerations.
  - **Trucks / Heavy Duty:** Cab height, aftermarket suspension lift clearances, empty bed requirement.
  - **Motorcycles & Snowmobiles:** Wheel chocks, palletized skids to protect carbide runners.
  - **Inoperables:** Winch cable deck loading requirements.

### Pillar 3: Risk Mitigation & Carrier Verification
- Remind users to verify broker/carrier active licensing on the FMCSA SAFER database.
- Advise them to request a Certificate of Insurance (COI) confirming primary cargo coverage ($100,000–$250,000).

### Pillar 4: Non-Spammy Contextual Reference
- Provide a helpful reference link to a specific calculator tool or deep-dive article on `skyautoservices.com` only when it directly answers the user's question.
- Never use promotional buzzwords ("best", "cheapest", "guaranteed #1"). Let technical competence establish brand authority.

## 4. Website & Marketing Parameters Checklist

Before any comment is approved or logged, it must satisfy all 5 validation checks:

- [x] **Math Accuracy:** Rate range matches `calculate_quote.php` baseline for that specific mileage and vehicle surcharge ($VF).
- [x] **Transit Time Realism:** Uses realistic industry timeframes (1–2 days for <500 mi, 3–4 days for 1,000 mi, 5–7 days for coast-to-coast).
- [x] **Tone Check:** Professional, empathetic, objective, and grammatically impeccable.
- [x] **Platform Compliance:** Conforms with Reddit Content Policy, vBulletin forum rules, and XenForo guidelines.
- [x] **Zero Drift / No Mock Data:** Contains only real-world facts, active domain URLs, and verified industry guidelines.
