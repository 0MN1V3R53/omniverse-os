Frequently Asked Questions

How is my shipping price calculated?+
Your quote is based on the real road distance between your pickup and delivery cities, your vehicle type, the transport type (open or enclosed), the unit's condition (operable or inoperable), and whether you're shipping personal items inside. Once you provide your VIN, our system pulls exact vehicle specs to finalize the rate. The longer the route, the lower the cost per mile.
Is my vehicle insured during transport?+
Yes. Every carrier in our network carries active cargo insurance, and we verify coverage before dispatch so your vehicle is protected door to door.
What's the difference between open and enclosed?+
Open transport is the most popular and affordable option — the same carriers dealerships use. Enclosed transport fully protects your vehicle from weather and road debris, ideal for classics, exotics and luxury cars.
How long does shipping take?+
Transit time depends on distance. As a rule of thumb, carriers cover roughly 400–500 miles per day on the road once your vehicle is picked up.
Can you ship any make or model?+
Absolutely — from a 1950s classic to the newest EV. Our quote tool includes every major make, model, body style and weight class so your estimate is accurate.
