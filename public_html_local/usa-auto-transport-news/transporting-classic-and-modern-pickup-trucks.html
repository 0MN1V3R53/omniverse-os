Transporting Classic & Modern Pickup Trucks Across State Lines

Marcus Sterling

Senior Logistics Analyst

Published

April 28, 2026

America loves pickup trucks, and shipping full-size pickups—from vintage square-body Chevy C10s to modern Ford F-150s and Ram 1500s—is a daily operation for nationwide auto haulers.

Carriers account for extended bed lengths, crew cab dimensions, and aftermarket camper shells when positioning trucks on multi-level trailers. Hard tonneau covers and bed toolboxes must be securely locked to prevent wind displacement during highway transit.

Professional carrier placement ensures balanced weight distribution and secure transit for heavy-duty and light-duty trucks alike.
