Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, insurance, transit times, and vehicle logistics.

Q:Is my vehicle fully insured during transport?

Yes. Every carrier in the Sky Auto Services network is strictly vetted and must maintain active primary cargo insurance ranging from $100,000 up to $1,000,000+ depending on trailer capacity. We verify Certificate of Insurance (COI) records directly with insurance underwriters prior to carrier assignment.

Q:Do you require an upfront deposit or booking fee?

No. Sky Auto Services maintains a strict $0 upfront deposit policy. You pay nothing to initiate a quote or schedule your transport corridor. Payment is only processed once your carrier is officially assigned, scheduled, and dispatched.

Q:What happens if I need to cancel my shipment?

You can cancel anytime free of charge before a carrier is formally dispatched to your vehicle. We believe in 100% risk-free vehicle logistics with no cancellation penalties or hidden processing fees.

Q:Can you transport inoperable, modified, or classic vehicles?

Yes. We regularly transport inoperable vehicles using specialized winch-equipped trailers and rollback ramps. For classic, exotic, and lowered sports cars, our enclosed transport service features hydraulic liftgates to guarantee zero undercarriage scraping.

Q:How long does auto transport take from pickup to delivery?

Transit times are based on road mileage: Regional trips (under 500 miles) typically take 1 to 2 days; Mid-distance corridors (500 to 1,500 miles) take 2 to 4 days; Cross-country routes (1,500 to 3,000+ miles) take 5 to 7 days. Our dispatch desk provides real-time progress updates.

Q:Is door-to-door auto transport included?

Yes. All standard quotes include direct door-to-door delivery. Your driver will pick up and deliver your vehicle as close to your physical address as legally and safely accessible for multi-car transport rigs.

How much do car transport companies charge?

Car shipping costs are based on several factors, including current market trends, carrier availability and fuel prices. Your total cost will also depend on the type of car you want to move, where it needs to go, its condition (operable or inoperable), whether you choose open or enclosed trailer, and finally, the time of year when you book.

🚗
Size and weight of vehicle
Larger, heavier vehicles take up more trailer space and cost more to ship.
›
🔧
The car condition
Inoperable vehicles require special equipment to load and unload, adding cost.
›
🚛
The transport type
Open transport is most affordable. Enclosed transport offers premium protection.
›
📍
The shipping distance
Longer routes cost more overall, but the cost-per-mile typically decreases.
›
📅
Pickup date and season
Snowbird seasons (fall/spring) drive higher demand and pricing across key corridors.
